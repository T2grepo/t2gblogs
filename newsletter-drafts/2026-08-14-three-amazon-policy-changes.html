Amazon shipped three separate changes to catalog, advertising, and creative rules within the same two weeks — and we've broken down exactly what each one means for your account.

Titles, Ad Billing, and AI Tags All Changed at Once

Product titles are now capped at 75 characters (down from ~200), and Amazon has already auto-rewritten over 984 million titles with its own AI — you get a 14-day window to review and fix any that dropped your primary keyword or brand name. Sponsored Ads billing now defaults to automatic deduction straight from your seller proceeds instead of a credit card, which changes your payout timing and amounts week to week. And any photorealistic AI-generated person in your product images or A+ Content now needs a specific disclosure tag, or you risk listing suppression. We walk through exactly what to check and fix for each one.

One Thing to Try This Week

Go to Ads Console → Billing → Payment Method and check whether you're now on automatic deduction from your seller proceeds. If that cash flow timing doesn't work for your business, switch to Pay by Invoice to revert to a monthly invoice cycle closer to the old credit card cadence — and re-forecast your Q3 disbursements either way.
