The New Placement Quietly Inflating ACOS

On August 10, Amazon began auto-enrolling active Sponsored Products campaigns set to "Increase reach" into a new creator-content placement — reviews and buying guides written by Amazon Influencer Program creators. No opt-in, and your Top-of-Search bid modifier doesn't carry over, so your search-calibrated bid is now paying for a completely untested placement. We break down exactly why that mechanism moves your numbers even when you haven't changed a thing, and why TACOS catches this kind of account-wide drift faster than campaign-by-campaign ACOS does.

One Thing to Try This Week

Pull your placement report and isolate creator-content spend, clicks, and conversions for the last week, separate from Top of Search and Rest of Search. If ACOS on that placement is meaningfully worse than your search placements, you've found your leak — and you can bid it down instead of letting it run at your search rate by accident.
