Why Keyword-Perfect Listings Are Starting to Lose

Amazon's shopping assistant now reasons over structured attributes — material, certifications, use case, compatibility — to decide whether your listing actually answers a shopper's question, not just whether it matches their keywords. Most AI listing generators are still optimized for the old target, so a compliant, keyword-covered draft can still get skipped if the attributes behind it are thin. We also cover a real compliance rule buried in Amazon's July AI-image update that's easy to miss if your creative workflow predates it, and an honest read on Amazon's new native Ads Agent — worth piloting, not worth betting your core account on yet.

One Thing to Try This Week

Pull your top 15-20 SKUs by revenue and check whether your bullets actually answer the kind of longer, intent-based question a shopper might ask an AI assistant — not just whether they're keyword-dense. If they don't have an answer in them, that's your rewrite priority list, not your lowest-traffic listings. It takes an afternoon and it's one of the highest-leverage catalog fixes you can make right now.
