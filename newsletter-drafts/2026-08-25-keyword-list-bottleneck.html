Your Keyword List Isn't the Bottleneck Anymore

Amazon's ad platform quietly shifted under sellers' feet this month, and we wanted to flag it before it shows up as a slow ACOS creep in your account. Here's what changed and what to do about it.

Here's what's inside:

  • Two platform shifts — Amazon's AI campaign builder went global, and product targeting overtook keywords as the more contested battleground.
  • One thing to try — a simple audit to see if your product-targeting and keyword campaigns are actually separated with independent bids.

Amazon Ads Moved. Did Your Campaign Structure?

Amazon expanded Ads Agent, its natural-language DSP campaign builder, to every region where DSP runs, and advertisers using it are seeing an average 18% drop in CPMs and 16% lower CPA. At the same time, Amazon's matching now leans heavily on browsing and purchase behavior, not just literal search terms — which means product targeting on detail pages now carries as much weight as your keyword list, if not more. In the full post we walk through five concrete structural changes, including why product targeting deserves its own dedicated campaigns and how often you should really be refreshing your negative keyword list under this new matching behavior.

One Thing to Try This Week

Check whether your product targeting is still buried inside an "Auto" campaign with no bid differentiation. If it is, pull it into its own manual campaign split into three buckets — direct competitor ASINs, complementary products, and your own underperforming listings — and bid each one independently, since a defensive bid on your own page should never share a budget line with an offensive bid on a competitor's.
