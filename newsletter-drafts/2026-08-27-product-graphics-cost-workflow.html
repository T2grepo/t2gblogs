A Leaner Way to Produce Product Graphics

One high-resolution hero shoot per product family can feed twenty listing variants in post-production instead of funding twenty separate shoots. AI-generated backgrounds and lifestyle scenes are a smart way to extend that photography — Amazon's own data shows AI-assisted lifestyle imagery driving 40%+ higher click-through on Sponsored Brands mobile ads — but the product itself still needs to be real photography, not AI-rendered. And if synthetic people appear anywhere in your image set, Amazon now requires an XMP metadata tag at export, applied catalog-wide regardless of where your team is based.
