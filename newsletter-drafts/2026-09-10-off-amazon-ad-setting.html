A New Amazon Ad Setting Is Quietly Reshaping Your ACOS

Amazon just auto-enrolled every Sponsored Products campaign into a new off-Amazon placement default, and most sellers haven't noticed yet. We broke down what changed and how to rebuild your campaign settings around it.

Here's what's inside:

  • A default you didn't choose — Amazon's new off-Amazon setting is already spending your budget, and your search term reports now include queries no shopper ever typed.
  • One action to take this week — turn an inherited default into a per-campaign decision before it quietly moves your ACOS.

The Off-Amazon Setting Nobody Opted Into

Amazon quietly added a "Settings for ads served off Amazon" toggle to every campaign, defaulted it to "Increase reach," and auto-enrolled your existing campaigns without asking. That default also injects Amazon-inferred search terms into your reports for impressions where no shopper actually typed anything — data that can quietly pollute your negative-keyword list if you mine it the old way. We also cover Amazon's new no-bid, ROAS-only auto-bidding beta, and why it deserves a controlled test on a couple of campaigns rather than a blanket switch.

One Thing to Try This Week

Open your campaign settings and check the off-Amazon toggle on every campaign — it's been set to "Increase reach" by default since launch. Flip it to "Limit off-Amazon spend" on your low-margin, efficiency-focused campaigns, and leave reach on only where wider visibility is genuinely worth it, like new launches or high-margin hero ASINs.
