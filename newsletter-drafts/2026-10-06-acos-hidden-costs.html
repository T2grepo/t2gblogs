Your ACOS Looks Fine. Your Margin Doesn't.

Two separate cost increases hit Amazon sellers this year, and neither one shows up cleanly on a standard ACOS dashboard. We broke down what changed and what to do about it.

  • A half-day exercise that surfaces unprofitable SKUs — rebuilding break-even ACOS with current fee tables routinely finds two or three SKUs losing money even at a "good" ACOS.

The Hidden Cost Your Dashboard Won't Show

Sponsored Products CPCs have climbed to roughly $1.18–$1.34 on average, up close to a third from two years ago — meaning flat ad spend now buys meaningfully less reach. At the same time, Amazon's January 15, 2026 FBA fee restructuring quietly added $0.00–$0.51 per unit depending on size tier, with small-standard items over $50 taking a 15.4% hit. Because ACOS only measures ad spend against ad-attributed sales, it never catches a fulfillment fee increase — so a seller can hold ACOS steady all year and still watch net margin bleed out one layer down. One Thing to Try This Week

Recalculate break-even ACOS per SKU using the current fee schedule: (price − COGS − current fulfillment fees − other fees) ÷ price. Any SKU where your actual ACOS is close to or above that number is losing you money right now, even if the campaign report calls it "efficient" — it's a half-day exercise that's worth doing before your next budget cycle.
