  • One move to make this week — a free Brand Analytics report that can show you exactly where you're overpaying to defend searches you should already be winning.

A Hidden Price Floor — and $20B in Question

On August 31, the FTC and 22 state attorneys general sued Amazon, alleging its "second-price" Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands auctions have quietly included an undisclosed proxy floor value since 2019 — inflating what more than a million advertisers actually paid. The complaint puts the total at over $20 billion extracted from advertisers, while Amazon's average CPC has climbed roughly 35% since 2023 to about $1.21. Whatever the lawsuit ultimately proves, the practical takeaway for sellers is the same: rising CPCs are increasingly a market condition to manage around, not a targeting mistake to fix with a few negative keywords. The full post breaks down five concrete moves — from a branded-term audit to reallocating budget toward conversion — to help protect your margin while the case plays out.

One Thing to Try This Week

Pull your Search Query Performance report from Brand Analytics and filter it to your own branded search terms.
