Rising ACOS Isn't Just a "You" Problem

Amazon's advertising revenue hit $19.8 billion last quarter, up 26% year over year — and buried in that growth is a more telling figure: ad revenue per paid unit sold rose roughly 8% versus a year ago. That means the same volume of sales is now generating more ad spend marketplace-wide, driven in part by Amazon steering advertiser demand into new inventory like live-sports placements. In the full post, we walk through how to tell whether your ACOS increase is market drift or your own inefficiency, and lay out five concrete moves — from placement-level bid splits to budget-pacing around high-competition windows — to bring your number back down anyway.

One Thing to Try This Week

Pull your placement report by campaign and set placement-specific bid modifiers instead of one flat base bid. Push harder where Top of Search or Product Pages are proven winners, and dial back or exclude placements — including off-Amazon creator content — that aren't converting for your catalog. It's a quick fix that regularly recovers a point or more of ACOS without losing sales volume.
