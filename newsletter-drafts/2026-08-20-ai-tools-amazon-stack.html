AI Is a First Draft, Not a Final Answer

AI listing generators are genuinely fast at producing a compliant structural draft — bullets, backend terms, and a title inside the 75-character limit Amazon now enforces across more than 984 million titles since June. What they still miss is keyword prioritization against real search volume, so route every draft through a human pass and leave listings that are already ranking well alone. On the ads side, Ads Agent is free and native, but a first-generation agent optimizing inside Amazon's black box is not the same as a tool tuned to your margin targets — pilot it on one or two low-stakes campaigns, not your core account. One Thing to Try This Week

Audit your live images and A+ Content for photorealistic AI-generated people. Any of them now need a "contains-synthetic-performer" tag embedded in the file's XMP metadata before upload — untagged ones are a policy violation today, not a future risk. Going forward, keep AI generation for backgrounds, props and scene composition, and use real photography (or properly tagged AI people) whenever a human appears with your product.
