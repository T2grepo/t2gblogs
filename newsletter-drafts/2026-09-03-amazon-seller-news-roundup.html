Financing Rules, Ranking, and Ads All Just Shifted

A contract amendment that took effect August 24 now explicitly blocks pledging your future Amazon payouts as loan collateral — a detail that reaches revenue-based financing and account-sale deals, not just outright account transfers. Separately, Amazon has fully retired the old Featured Offer eligibility gate: your chargeback rate, order defect rate, and VOC complaints no longer work as a pass/fail checkpoint, they're now blended directly into your Buy Box ranking score. And Amazon's new Alexa+ Agentic Ads format is letting shoppers buy without ever tapping a screen, with more detail expected at unBoxed 2026 in late September. With the old Featured Offer eligibility gate gone, sellers who used to get excluded outright over a rough ODR or VOC patch are now back in the Buy Box rotation on ASINs you may have stopped watching.
