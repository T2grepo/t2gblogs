Amazon's New Free AI Creative Tools: Where to Actually Use Them

Amazon just pushed two new free AI creative tools into Seller Central and Creative Studio — and the forum advice to "just use the free tool" is, as usual, only half right. We broke down where these tools actually save money and where they can quietly cost you conversions.

Here's what's inside:

  • What actually shipped — a conversational concepting partner in Creative Studio and a native image generator built straight into the listing workflow.
  • One concrete move to make this week — tier your catalog before you touch either tool, so "free" goes where it actually pays off.

Free AI Tools Won't Fix a Broken Creative Workflow

At Accelerate 2026, Amazon added a chat-based concepting layer to Creative Studio that researches your product and audience and builds a storyboard before anything renders — the exact step agencies usually bill by the hour for. At the same time, a native AI image generator is rolling into Seller Central for basic background and lifestyle shots, built to never violate Amazon's own image requirements. Neither tool replaces a real creative workflow on its own; the real opportunity is reallocating your budget around them, not bolting them onto what you're already doing. We also cover the compliance tagging step (the `contains-synthetic-performer` XMP requirement) that's easy to miss when you add new tools to the pipeline.

One Thing to Try This Week

Tier your catalog before you touch any AI tool. Run the free native Seller Central image generator against your long-tail, low-volume SKUs first — that's where "free" actually saves money. Keep your top 15–20% of revenue-driving SKUs on human-directed photography or premium AI tools where precision and conversion matter most.
