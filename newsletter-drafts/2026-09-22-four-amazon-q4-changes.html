Four Q4 Changes Worth Your Attention Now

Amazon's Price History tool now shows a full year of pricing instead of just 90 days, which means pre-promotion markups used to inflate discount percentages are now visible to any shopper — and to Alexa for Shopping. The Q4 peak fulfillment surcharge window opens October 15 and runs through mid-January, and it hits differently by size tier, so a blanket estimate across your catalog won't cut it. Seller Fulfilled Prime's delivery-speed thresholds quietly went up in July, and Amazon measures them by page views rather than completed deliveries — meaning a rough shipping week can knock you below the line even when most orders arrive on time.

One Thing to Try This Week

Pull your average per-unit fulfillment cost for the October 15–January 14 surcharge window and rebuild your Q4 contribution margin SKU by SKU, not with a blanket catalog-wide estimate. Flag any SKUs that go from profitable to marginal once the surcharge is included, and decide now — price adjustment, paused ad spend, or accepted thinner margins — rather than reacting to it on your first November settlement.
