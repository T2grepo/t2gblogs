Amazon Just Replaced Its Whole Ad Console With AI

At unBoxed 2026, Amazon tore up the Ads Console and rebuilt it around a conversational "Ads Agent" and just three campaign types. We broke down what's actually in it and what we think sellers should do about it this week.

Here's what's inside:

  • What actually changed — a new $100/day Full-Funnel format, a merged DVA+ campaign type, and a chat agent that plans media across 20 marketplaces.
  • One move to make this week — how to test Full-Funnel Campaigns without risking your best-converting ASINs.

Three Campaign Types Just Replaced the Whole Console

Amazon collapsed the Ads Console and standalone DSP into "Amazon Ads Agent," organized around three campaign types instead of the old sprawl of formats. Full-Funnel Campaigns bundle Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, and streaming video into one AI-run object — but it requires a $100/day minimum and optimizes toward new-to-brand customers and "Long-Term Sales," not ACOS. DVA+ merges Sponsored Display, Sponsored TV, and programmatic buying, with both a hands-off simplified mode and an advanced mode that keeps your manual targeting and bid controls intact. One Thing to Try This Week

Before you let Full-Funnel Campaigns anywhere near your top sellers, run it on a secondary ASIN with steady conversion history instead — one you can afford to watch for three weeks. Set up a control Sponsored Products campaign alongside it and compare against your normal ACOS benchmarks, not just the new-to-brand and Long-Term Sales metrics Amazon's AI is optimizing toward, before deciding it's additive rather than just spending down budget your proven keywords used to get.
